# GRE SE词汇 + 真题解析（0628-0701）

## SE词汇详解

**invidious** /ɪnˈvɪdiəs/ 令人反感的、不公平且招人反感的
≠ envious（嫉妒别人），invidious是「让别人嫉妒/反感」
- invidious comparison（捧一踩一的比较）
- invidious position（双重角色冲突→怎么做都招致不满）
- invidious distinction（不合理区别对待）
同义≈ objectionable/unfair/discriminatory

**perfunctory** /pərˈfʌŋktəri/ 敷衍的、走过场的
核心：为了应付而做，缺乏投入
- 场景：glance at title page before signing off → perfunctory
- 场景：going through the motions → perfunctory smile
同义≈ cursory/superficial/half-hearted/mechanical
反义≠ thorough/meticulous/painstaking/conscientious

**unfounded + unjustified** — SE常配对
- unfounded（没事实根据）≈ unjustified（没道理）
- 场景：pessimism turned out to be unfounded/unjustified
- 区别：日常使用中unfounded偏无事实，unjustified偏无道理

## 0629真题 考满分5题（Q28-Q32 · 全hard）

Q28. investors grateful AG stepped in, given that regulators have been _______.
→ A) **diffident** ✅（畏缩）| E) tenacious ❌（顽强→逻辑反了）

Q29. While writer best known for much-ballyhooed _______, her impact reached far beyond memorable quips.
→ B) **drollness** ✅（滑稽幽默↔️quips对应）

Q30. SE: It's hardly _______ the committee calls for: rudimentary competence would be an improvement.
→ **faultlessness + perfection** ✅ | accountability ❌（不是完美维度）

Q31. The artist's photos are politically charged, yet it would be wrong to characterize them as _______.
→ A) **polemical** ✅（好辩的/有立场的）

Q32. SE: Even if the story seems innocuous, it _______ some key traits of those bleaker tales.
→ **anticipates + prefigures** ✅（预示）| possesses ❌

## 0630 张巍第一组（5题）

① Easy: science is not _______ enterprise; indeed, it is quite the opposite, a motley assortment.
→ **monolithic** ✅（单一的↔️大杂烩）

② SE: artists escaped chaos, looked to nature for _______ and serenity.
→ **order + stability** ✅

③ SE: nothingness from _______ concept to something we can put under a microscope.
→ **nebulous + vague** ✅ | tangible/palpable方向反了

④ SE: everyday domestic life largely _______ other media.
→ **left to + ceded to** ✅ | substituted for方向反了

⑤ SE: charge does not _______ his moderate political record.
→ **conform to + square with** ✅

## 0701 张巍第二组（5题）

① Medieval cathedrals are marvels, but medieval physics is the _______ of what science ought to be.
→ **antithesis** ✅（反面/对立面）| apotheosis/archetype/paradigm/culmination都是正面

② The senator's speech was surprisingly _______; rather than bombastic rhetoric, she spoke with directness.
→ **unpretentious** ✅（不矫饰的）

③ SE: Poaching → survival _______ ; indeed, anything but secure.
→ **precarious + perilous** ✅

④ SE: Unlike predecessor's expansive vision, new director's agenda was _______.
→ **confined + circumscribed** ✅

⑤ SE: not merely critical; filled with _______, targeting character as well.
→ **animadversion** ✅（批评/非难——criticism升级版）

## 词汇表
- diffident — 不自信的、畏缩的
- drollness — 滑稽幽默
- faultlessness — 完美无缺（= perfection）
- polemical — 好辩的、有立场的
- anticipates — 预示（= prefigures）
- antithesis — 对立面
- unpretentious — 不矫饰的
- precarious — 岌岌可危的（= perilous）
- confined — 有限的（= circumscribed）
- animadversion — 批评、非难
