# GRE TC — Q1-Q27 倒装·转折·递进·对比 四大逻辑

## 倒装 🔄
1. ❌ Particularly suspect in the prosecutor's case **was** the _______ testimony of the sole eyewitness.
→ **unreliable** | 表语前置：形+was+名 → 主语在后

3. ❌ More _______ than the commissioner's ruling **was** the public's indifference to it; the decision was met with a collective shrug.
→ **disheartening** | 还原：Y was more 形 than X

4. ✅ **So** _______ **was** the ambassador's statement **that** it required three subsequent clarifications.
→ **incendiary** | so...that倒装：So+形+was+主+that...

5. ❌ The historian's account was **so** _______ **that** even scholars discovered new details.
→ **exhaustive** | so...that因果

11. ❌ **Especially** _______ **is** the argument that economic growth alone will remedy inequality, **given that** decades of expansion have only widened the gap.
→ **tenuous** | 还原：The argument is especially...

14. ✅ **So** pervasive **was** the culture of secrecy **that** whistleblowers found themselves ostracized.
→ **was** | 主谓一致看主语 the culture

22. ✅ **So** _______ **was** the company's quarterly report **that** even optimistic analysts revised projections downward.
→ **disappointing**

## 转折 ↔️
2. ❌ **Although** the architect's design was widely _______ for its boldness, **critics argued** it sacrificed functionality.
→ **acclaimed** | although → critics负面 → blank必正面

7. ❌ **Even** the most _______ observer would have struggled to detect the subtle forgery.
→ **seasoned** | even让步：连最X都看不出 → X取极端值（suspicious的人反而更易看出，方向反）

9. ✅ **Although** the CEO's public statements projected confidence, her private emails revealed a deeply _______ outlook.
→ **pessimistic**

13. ❌ **Far from** being a _______ of tradition, the composer's later works were **in fact** deeply rooted in classical forms.
→ **repudiation** | far from X...in fact Y

15. ❌ **Despite** the candidate's _______ record on environmental issues, many voters remained skeptical.
→ **impeccable** | unwavering修饰commitment/stance，不搭配record

18. ✅ **Although** the drug showed great promise in early trials, its long-term side effects proved too _______ for approval.
→ **severe** | too severe for approval

19. ❌ **Although** the scientist's theory was initially _______ by the academic community, subsequent experiments confirmed its validity → Nobel.
→ **dismissed** | 转折选最强反差：dismissed↔️获奖

23. ✅ **Though** the documentary claimed to offer an objective account, its selection of interviews was far from _______.
→ **impartial** | far from+褒义词=实际反面

24. ✅ **Despite** the neighborhood's lingering reputation for _______, it has become increasingly varied.
→ **homogeneity** | despite → increasingly varied → X=不多样

25. ✅ **Although** Pauline Kael had a distaste for sycophancy, she also had a need for (i)_______; as a consequence, she sent very (ii)_______ signals.
→ (i) **flattery** | (ii) **contradictory**

## 递进 ➡️
8. ❌ The memoir was criticized for its _______ tone; the author seemed **incapable of acknowledging** any mistake.
→ **self-justifying** | ;后直接解释：不承认错误

10. ✅ not merely selective...it was **downright** _______
→ **fraudulent** | not merely X → downright Y：Y是X的极端版

16. ✅ not merely inaccurate...it was _______, fabricating data.
→ **fraudulent** | 同Q10逻辑

21. ❌ The ambassador's statement was **not merely** provocative; it was _______, threatening to unravel diplomatic progress.
→ **incendiary** | reckless跟provocative非同维度；incendiary是provocative的极端版

26. ❌ claims were accepted as true if reasonable; experimental verification, if thought necessary at all, was _______.
→ **perfunctory** | 前科学时代——实验验证只是走过场

## 对比 🎭
6. ✅ The company's commitment to sustainability **proved** _______ **when** a memo revealed plans to circumvent regulations.
→ **illusory** | 时间对比：前期承诺 vs 后期违规

12. ❌ **Where** her predecessor was known for _______ decrees, the new director made every decision through consensus-building.
→ **peremptory** | where对比：predecessor独断 vs new共识

17. ❌ **Where** previous administration pursued _______, the new government embraced protectionist measures.
→ **engagement** | hostility(敌意)和protectionism同向，非对比

20. ✅ **Where** the author's earlier novels were praised for _______ prose, her later work was criticized for being overwrought.
→ **restrained** | elaborate/flamboyant/baroque都跟later同向

27. ✅ **Instead of** demonstrating the _______ of archaeological applications, the study became an illustration of imprudence.
→ **promise** | Instead of X → became Y(负面)：X必正面
