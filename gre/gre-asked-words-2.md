# GRE 单词学习记录

## infraction / affliction 区分笔记

**对比：**

| | infraction | affliction |
|---|---|---|
| 词根 | *frangere*（打破）→ 打破规则 | *fligere*（打击）→ 被命运打击 |
| 核心义 | 违规（违反规则/法律） | 苦难（身心的持续痛苦） |
| 典型搭配 | *minor infraction, traffic infraction* | *chronic affliction, suffered from affliction* |
| 反义词 | compliance, observance | relief, comfort |

**快速记忆：**
- **infraction** = in（进入）+ **fract**（打破）→ 打破了规则 → 违规
- **affliction** = ad（向）+ **flict**（打击）→ 被打了 → 苦难

**例句：**
- *The principal considered the student's repeated tardiness a minor **infraction** of school policy.*（违反校规）
- *She regarded her chronic back pain as a lifelong **affliction**.*（长期病痛）
