# GRE 单词学习记录

## ubiquitous

**释义：** 无处不在的，普遍存在的 — 形容事物分布极广、到处都能见到

**词根：** Latin *ubique* = everywhere。同根词：**ubiquity** (n. 普遍存在)

**场景（GRE 常考）：**
- 科技/数据 — smartphones / social media are ubiquitous in modern life，描述技术渗透
- 阅读社会现象 — 某个趋势或问题 becomes ubiquitous，形容已从个别蔓延到全社会
- 填空对比 — ubiquitous ⟷ rare/scarce，普遍 vs 稀有，反义对立；也考 ubiquitous ≠ novel（普遍 ≠ 新奇）

**画面：** CCTV cameras in London are so ubiquitous that the average commuter is captured on film over 300 times a day — yet no one even notices them anymore.

**Synonyms:** omnipresent, pervasive, universal, prevalent, ever-present
**Antonyms:** rare, scarce, uncommon, absent, isolated

**区分：**
- ubiquitous — 强调「到处都有」，中性/客观描述
- pervasive — 「弥漫渗透」，偏负面（pervasive corruption），不只空间上普遍，还强调深入渗透
- omnipresent — 「无所不在」，带宗教/哲学色彩，GRE 一般不考
- prevalent — 「盛行」，偏统计数据上占多数，不是空间上到处可见

## sift

**释义：** v. 仔细筛选、甄别；过滤（从大量信息中筛出重要部分）

**词根：** 古英语 *siftan*（筛），与 sieve（筛子）同源

**场景：**
- **阅读 — 学术论证：** *sift through the evidence / data* — 研究人员从海量数据中筛选有用信息
- **填空 — 逻辑对立：** *sift fact from fiction* — 从谣言中甄别真相，常与 discern、winnow 形成同义逻辑

**画面：** 考古学家跪在尘土中用小刷子 *sift* 每一寸沙土，只为找出那颗米粒大小的化石碎片。

**Synonyms:** filter, winnow, screen, scrutinize
**Antonyms:** lump together, jumble, amalgamate

**区分：** *sift* vs *winnow* — 二者都是"筛选"，但 sift 强调「筛出重要/相关的」，winnow 强调「剔除不重要/无关的」（winnow out the chaff from the grain）。GRE 填空中常互为同义词。

## mull

**释义：** v. 仔细琢磨、反复思考（长时间地）

**词根：** 中古英语 *mullen*（研磨成粉），引申为「在脑中反复研磨」

**场景：**
- **阅读 — 人物刻画：** *mull over a decision / problem* — 角色陷入沉思，犹豫不决
- **填空 — 时间逻辑：** *mull for weeks before...* — 与 impetuous、hasty 形成对立，强调深思熟虑

**画面：** 他捧着咖啡在窗边站了半小时，*mulling* 要不要辞职去环游世界，咖啡都凉透了还没决定。

**Synonyms:** ponder, ruminate, contemplate, deliberate
**Antonyms:** dismiss, ignore, disregard

**区分：** *mull* vs *ruminate* — 几乎同义，但 ruminate 原意"反刍"，更强调反复咀嚼同一个想法；mull 更偏「犹豫中的思考」。*mull* vs *muse* — muse 是轻松的遐想，mull 是认真的权衡。

## effluvium / effluvia

**释义：** 恶臭、臭气；有害散发物（气体、气味等）。effluvia 是复数形式。GRE 中只考负面含义，指腐烂/工业/生物过程散发的难闻气味或有害排放。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *effluvium* ← *effluere*（流出）= *ex-*（出）+ *fluere*（流）→ 同根词：effluent（废水）、fluent（流利）、fluid（流体）、flux（流动）、influx（涌入）、confluence（汇合）、superfluous（多余的，字面义"溢出的"）

**场景：**
- **环境/生态（GRE阅读高频）：** 工业 effluvium 污染 vs. 原始荒野 pristine wilderness — 真题中直接出现 "removed from humans' modern effluvium" 指远离人类排放的偏远地区
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空）：** 某小镇空气 consistently _____，随风吹来啤酒厂的 sour effluvia、煤厂的 choking fumes、河边的 brackish smells → 答案形容"充满各种臭气"（真题出现）
- **社会批评/人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** 比喻用法 — "the effluvium of corruption" 腐败的臭气，用来刻画堕落氛围

**画面：** The effluvium from the tannery was so eye-wateringly foul that even the stray dogs, who ate garbage for a living, crossed to the other side of the street.

**Synonyms:** stench, miasma, reek, emanation, noxious fumes, putrid odor
**Antonyms:** fragrance, perfume, aroma, scent, bouquet

**区分：** effluent（具体指废水/污水，液态排放）| miasma（瘴气，侧重致病传说）| emanation（中性散发物，不带贬义）

## disgruntled

**释义：** 不满的、怨愤的、恼怒的。因未得应得之物或受不公正对待而产生的怨恨不满。注意：dis- 不是否定前缀，是加强语气——"极度抱怨"。

**词根：** gruntle（古英语，咕哝、抱怨）← grunt（哼哼声）。gruntled（满意的）已死亡，只剩加强版的 disgruntled。同源：grunt、grudge（怨恨）。

**场景：**
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读高频）：** "disgruntled workforce threatening to strike" — 刻画群体怨气和社会张力
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空）：** disgruntled vs. contented / placated / mollified — 经典对立
- **职场/组织行为（GRE阅读）：** "disgruntled shareholders"、"disgruntled former employees" — 公司治理、股东维权语境

**画面：** The disgruntled postman, after being barked at by the same Chihuahua for the 847th time, seriously considered whether federal mail-tampering laws applied to tiny yappy dogs.

**Synonyms:** discontented, dissatisfied, resentful, aggrieved, displeased, vexed, malcontent
**Antonyms:** content, satisfied, pleased, gratified, mollified, placated

**区分：** disenchanted（幻灭，理想破灭）| disaffected（对体制疏离，政治化）| disconsolate（极度悲伤，非愤怒）

## ruminate

**释义：** 反复思考、沉思、反复琢磨（GRE主考义）；反刍。GRE几乎只考比喻义——过度反复纠结的负面思考模式。

**词根：** 拉丁语 ruminatus ← ruminare（反刍）← rumen（瘤胃）。同根词：ruminant（反刍动物；沉思的）、rumination（沉思；反刍）。

**场景：**
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读高频）：** "he ruminated endlessly on every perceived slight" — 刻画敏感/偏执/抑郁的角色
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空）：** ruminate vs. act decisively / dismiss lightly — 反复纠结↔果断，反复琢磨↔轻率放过
- **心理学语境（GRE阅读）：** rumination as cognitive pattern — 抑郁症 tendency to ruminate vs. healthy distraction

**画面：** After the interview, he ruminated on whether he'd shaken hands too firmly for the rest of the weekend, replaying the three-second interaction in his head approximately 400 times.

**Synonyms:** ponder, contemplate, mull over, deliberate, brood, dwell on, chew over, obsess over
**Antonyms:** dismiss, ignore, disregard, brush off, shrug off

**区分：** mull（中性"仔细考虑"）| deliberate（理性、有目的权衡）| ruminate 独有"反刍"意象，带 obsessive 负面色彩

## mollify

**释义：** 安抚、平息、使缓和。专门针对愤怒/不满情绪——通过让步、解释或安慰让对方平静下来。源自"使变软"。

**词根：** 拉丁语 mollificare = mollis（软的）+ facere（使…）= "使变软"。同根词：emollient（润肤剂；安抚的）、mollusk（软体动物）、mollification（安抚）。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空高频）：** mollify vs. aggravate/provoke/enrage — 安抚↔激怒，经典反义对。真题常见：某人的道歉不仅没能 mollify 对方，反而_____了局势
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "executives scrambled to mollify outraged customers" — 危机公关/卑微讨好
- **政治/外交（GRE阅读）：** mollify critics/opponents/public opinion — 为平息公众情绪而让步

**画面：** He attempted to mollify his furious girlfriend with a hastily purchased bouquet, but having brought carnations — her least favorite flower, associated with a traumatic prom night — he somehow made the situation dramatically worse.

**Synonyms:** appease, pacify, placate, soothe, calm, assuage, conciliate, propitiate
**Antonyms:** aggravate, provoke, enrage, inflame, antagonize, exacerbate

**区分：** placate（强调让步/妥协）| assuage（可减轻饥饿/痛苦/欲望，范围更广）| mitigate（减轻客观严重程度，非情绪）| mollify 精确针对愤怒和不满

**另见：** disgruntled（反义词）

## even-tempered

**释义：** 性情平和的、不易怒的、沉稳的。无论外界如何波动，始终保持稳定情绪——不轻易发火，也不过度兴奋。

**词根：** even（平稳、均匀）+ tempered（有…脾气的）。同根词：temperament（气质）、temperance（节制）、temper（脾气；回火）。

**场景：**
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读高频）：** "an even-tempered negotiator who never rose to the bait" — 对比周围慌乱，突出其沉稳
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空）：** even-tempered vs. volatile/mercurial/irascible — 沉稳↔暴躁/多变，经典反义对
- **职场/管理（GRE阅读）：** "her even-tempered leadership steadied the company through the crisis"

**画面：** While the rest of the team sprinted around like headless chickens when the server crashed mid-demo, the even-tempered CTO took a calm sip of coffee and said, "Let's try the backup," as if someone had merely spilled a little milk.

**Synonyms:** equable, calm, composed, placid, serene, unflappable, imperturbable, equanimous
**Antonyms:** volatile, mercurial, irascible, temperamental, choleric, hot-headed, excitable

**区分：** phlegmatic（迟钝/冷漠，偏负面）| stoic（忍受痛苦，不单是情绪）| equable（更正式，GRE更喜欢）

## acquaintance

**释义：** 熟人（非密友）；了解、认识（对某事物的粗浅知识）。GRE 重点不在"熟人"日常义，而在知识深度的梯度——与 mastery/intimacy 形成对立。

**词根：** 古法语 acointier ← 拉丁语 accognitus = ad-（朝向）+ cognitus（已知）← cognoscere（知道）。同根词：cognition（认知）、cognizant（知晓的）、recognize（认出）、incognito（隐姓埋名的）、connoisseur（行家）。

**场景：**
- **阅读学术论证（GRE阅读高频）：** "passing acquaintance with statistics" = 对统计学的粗浅了解 — 与 deep expertise 对比，质疑观点可信度
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空）：** acquaintance with vs. mastery of / deep knowledge of — 粗浅了解↔精通，常考"她以为自己很懂，其实只是_____"双空题
- **人物关系（GRE阅读）：** acquaintance vs. confidant/intimate — 熟人 vs. 密友，刻画社交距离

**画面：** He claimed "more than a passing acquaintance" with French cuisine, but his credentials boiled down to having eaten a croissant once in 2019.

**Synonyms:** familiarity（粗浅了解时）、awareness、superficial knowledge、nodding acquaintance
**Antonyms:** mastery、expertise、intimacy、deep knowledge、ignorance（完全不知）

**区分：** familiarity（可深可浅）| intimacy（深度了解/亲密）| acquaintance 一定是粗浅的。固定搭配 "passing acquaintance" = superficial knowledge，GRE高频。

## felicitous

**释义：** （措辞/选择）恰当的、贴切的、得体的。专指用词、比喻或行动恰到好处——不多不少，刚好对味。GRE 特别注意 happy 作此义时的用法。

**词根：** 拉丁语 felix（幸福的、幸运的）→ felicitas（幸福；妙语）→ felicitous。同根词：felicity（幸福；措辞恰当）、infelicitous（不恰当的）、felicitate（祝贺）。

**场景：**
- **措辞/修辞（GRE填空高频）：** "a felicitous turn of phrase" — 用词精妙、一语中的。对立词是 awkward/inept/maladroit
- **学术论证（GRE阅读）：** "a felicitous example" — 恰到好处的例子，精准支持论点，与 contrived example（牵强的例子）对比
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "known for felicitous remarks at dinner parties" — 善于得体发言，社交金句王

**画面：** His comparison of the micromanaging boss to a seagull — swooping in, making noise, crapping on everything, then flying away — was so felicitous that even HR had to stifle a laugh.

**Synonyms:** apt, appropriate, fitting, well-chosen, opportune, propitious, happy（作"恰当的"时）
**Antonyms:** infelicitous, awkward, inept, inappropriate, maladroit, unfortunate, clumsy

**区分：** fortuitous（偶然发生的，中性）| auspicious（预示未来成功的）| happy 在 GRE 中常考"恰当的"义：a happy choice of words = 措辞恰当，不是"快乐的选择"

## vicious

**释义：** 凶残的、残暴的；恶性的（vicious cycle/circle 恶性循环）；恶毒的。GRE 最常考 vicious cycle 固定搭配，与 virtuous cycle（良性循环）形成对立。

**词根：** 拉丁语 vitiosus（有缺陷的）← vitium（缺陷、恶习）。同根词：vice（恶习）、vitiate（使失效、败坏）、vicious circle（恶性循环）。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立（GRE填空高频）：** vicious cycle vs. virtuous cycle — 恶性循环↔良性循环。真题：poverty creates a _____ cycle in which lack of education leads to low income, which prevents investment in education
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "a vicious personal attack" — 恶毒的人身攻击，刻画反派
- **系统/机制（GRE阅读）：** "vicious feedback loop" — 恶性反馈循环，经济/生态/社会政策分析常见

**画面：** The vicious cycle was a work of art: anxiety caused insomnia, insomnia tanked his performance, poor performance fueled more anxiety, and by Thursday he was running purely on espresso and regret.

**Synonyms:** brutal, savage, cruel, ferocious, malevolent, malignant
**Antonyms:** benign, virtuous, gentle, kind, benevolent

**区分：** ⚠️ vicious vs. viscous（粘稠的）— 经典拼写陷阱 | vitriolic（专指言语尖酸）| vicious circle vs. vicious cycle 均可，circle 更传统/学术

## unfathomable / inscrutable

两个都是"无法理解"，但底层意象不同——GRE 喜欢用这对同义词在填空中互相替换或区分。

**unfathomable （adj.）**
**释义：** 深不可测的、无法理解的。字面义"无法测量深度的"——fathom 是英寻（水深单位 ≈ 1.8m），所以 unfathomable = 探不到底。

**词根：** un-（不）+ fathom（英寻；理解、探测）+ -able。fathom 作动词 = to understand，来自"用测深绳探测水深"的意象。同源：fathomless。

**场景：**
- **宇宙/哲学（GRE阅读）：** "the universe's unfathomable vastness" — 浩瀚到超越人类认知极限
- **填空逻辑对立：** unfathomable vs. comprehensible/explicable — 不可理解↔可解释

**inscrutable （adj.）**
**释义：** 难以捉摸的、高深莫测的。字面义"无法审视的"——scrutari = 仔细检查，所以 inscrutable = 看透了也读不懂。

**词根：** 拉丁语 inscrutabilis = in-（不）+ scrutari（仔细检查、搜寻）。同根词：scrutiny（仔细审查）、scrutinize（仔细检查）。

**场景：**
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读高频）：** "his inscrutable expression betrayed nothing" — 扑克脸，读不出任何信息
- **填空逻辑对立：** inscrutable vs. transparent/readable — 高深莫测↔一目了然
- **东方主义语境（GRE阅读）：** "the inscrutable East" — 西方视角下"神秘莫测的东方"，经典文化批评考点

**核心区分：** unfathomable 意象是海洋"深"到探不到底（适用深度/复杂度：mystery、universe），inscrutable 意象是面具"密"到看不透（适用表面/表情：expression、face、motives）。反义词：unfathomable ↔ comprehensible；inscrutable ↔ transparent。

**画面（合体）：** The detective stared at the suspect's inscrutable poker face — impossible to read, as always — and wondered what unfathomable depths of stupidity could have led him to commit the crime on camera.

**Synonyms:** incomprehensible, impenetrable, enigmatic, mysterious, opaque, arcane
**Antonyms:** comprehensible, transparent, readable, fathomable, scrutable（罕见）

## scrupulous

**释义：** 一丝不苟的、严谨认真的；有道德操守的、审慎的。两层含义紧密结合——做事极度认真且有原则底线。注意反义词 unscrupulous（不择手段的）在 GRE 中出现频率更高。

**词根：** 拉丁语 scrupulus（小尖石 → 鞋里的石子让人不安）→ scrupulosus（焦虑的、小心的）。同根词：scruple（n.顾虑、良心不安；v.有顾虑）、unscrupulous（不择手段的）。

**场景：**
- **研究/学术（GRE填空高频）：** "scrupulous attention to detail" / "scrupulous methodology" — 一丝不苟的研究方法，与 slipshod/careless 对立
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "a scrupulous accountant who never cut corners" — 绝不偷工减料的角色，对比 unscrupulous profiteers
- **道德判断（GRE阅读）：** "she was scrupulous about citing sources" — 引用极为谨慎，学术诚信语境

**画面：** The scrupulous auditor noticed a one-cent discrepancy in the expense report and, three weeks later, had unraveled a $4 million embezzlement scheme that the less scrupulous accountants had been ignoring for years.

**Synonyms:** meticulous, conscientious, fastidious, punctilious, painstaking, exacting, principled
**Antonyms:** unscrupulous, careless, slipshod, negligent, remiss, cavalier

**区分：** meticulous（纯细节控，中性）| fastidious（可挑剔到烦人，偏负面）| scrupulous 独有道德审慎底色（正面）。⚠️ unscrupulous 比 scrupulous 更常在 GRE 中出现，记反义词更快！

## provenance

**释义：** 来源、出处、流转历史。专指物品（尤指艺术品/古董）的来源地和流转记录。GRE 艺术史类阅读高频词。

**词根：** 法语 provenance ← 拉丁语 provenire（生出、起源）= pro-（向前）+ venire（来）。同根词：venue（场地）、advent（到来）、convene（召集）、intervene（干预）、circumvent（规避）。

**场景：**
- **艺术/古董（GRE阅读高频）：** "the painting's provenance was traced back to a 17th-century Florentine workshop" — 来源考证，与 authenticity 紧密关联。没有 provenance 的藏品易被怀疑为伪作
- **学术论证（GRE阅读）：** "the provenance of the data was suspect" — 数据来源不明，质疑研究可信度
- **填空逻辑：** 验证 provenance → 确定 authenticity；lacking provenance → dubious/spurious

**画面：** The auction house couldn't verify the painting's provenance, which was especially awkward given the seller's claim that it had been "in the family ever since Napoleon personally handed it to my ancestor, a humble cheese merchant from Normandy who happened to be passing through Waterloo."

**Synonyms:** origin, source, derivation, pedigree, lineage, birthplace
**Antonyms:** destination, terminus

**区分：** ⚠️ provenance vs. providence（天意/神佑）— 经典拼写陷阱，差一个字母意思完全不同 | pedigree（限血统/品系纯正）| provenience（考古学变体，GRE不考）

## facile

**释义：** 轻率的、肤浅的；轻而易举的。GRE 重点考"过于简单化/浮于表面"的贬义——仅看表面就下结论。

**词根：** 拉丁语 facilis（容易的）← facere（做）。同根词：facility、facilitate。⚠️ 与"容易"同源，但 GRE 中贬义用法 >> 中性"容易"。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑：** facile explanation/argument = 肤浅的解释，禁不起推敲。对立词：thorough、rigorous、profound
- **人物刻画：** facile optimism = 盲目的乐观，不切实际

**Synonyms:** superficial, simplistic, shallow, glib
**Antonyms:** profound, thorough, rigorous, deep

---

## intransigent

**释义：** 不妥协的、不让步的。极度固执，拒绝任何让步或协商。

**词根：** 西班牙语 intransigente ← in-（不）+ transigir（妥协）。同根词：transigent（愿意妥协的，罕见）。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** intransigent vs. conciliatory/accommodating — 不妥协↔和解。常与 uncompromising、recalcitrant、obdurate 形成同义链
- **谈判/政治：** intransigent negotiating position — 铁板一块的立场

**Synonyms:** uncompromising, obdurate, recalcitrant, unyielding, adamant
**Antonyms:** conciliatory, accommodating, flexible, yielding

---

## obdurate

**释义：** 固执的、执拗的；铁石心肠的。比 stubborn 更正式，带道德判断——明知不对也不改。

**词根：** 拉丁语 obduratus = ob-（加强）+ durus（硬的）→ "彻底硬化"。同根词：durable、endure、duration。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** obdurate vs. amenable/tractable — 固执↔顺从。经典反义对
- **人物刻画：** obdurate refusal = 顽固拒绝，铁了心不改

**Synonyms:** stubborn, intransigent, recalcitrant, unyielding, obstinate
**Antonyms:** amenable, tractable, compliant, yielding

---

## vacuous

**释义：** 空洞的、无思想的、茫然的。形容内容贫乏或表情呆滞——vacuous stare（茫然凝视）是经典搭配。

**词根：** 拉丁语 vacuus（空的）→ vacuum（真空）。同根词：vacuum、evacuate、vacant。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑：** vacuous remark = 空洞的言论，毫无实质。对立词：substantive、meaningful
- **人物刻画：** vacuous expression = 茫然的表情，脑子一片空白

**Synonyms:** empty, hollow, inane, mindless, blank
**Antonyms:** substantive, meaningful, profound, thoughtful

---

## imperturbable

**释义：** 沉着冷静的、不为所动的。无论外界多混乱，内心稳如磐石——比 calm 更正式、更极致。

**词根：** 拉丁语 imperturbabilis = im-（不）+ perturbare（扰乱）。同根词：perturb（扰乱）、turbulent（动荡的）。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** imperturbable vs. flustered/agitated — 沉着↔慌乱
- **人物刻画：** imperturbable demeanor = 泰山崩于前而色不变
- **易混淆：** unflappable（同义，更口语化）

**Synonyms:** unflappable, composed, serene, unruffled, equanimous
**Antonyms:** flustered, agitated, perturbed, rattled

---

## divulge

**释义：** 泄露、透露（机密/隐私）。强调把本该保密的信息说出去——不是中性的"分享"，而是"不该说却说"。

**词根：** 拉丁语 divulgare（公之于众）= di-（广泛、分散）+ vulgare（使大众知晓）← vulgus（普通民众、大众）。同根词：vulgar（粗俗的，字面义"大众的"）、vulgarity（粗俗）、divulgence（泄露）。

**场景：**
- **保密/机密（GRE填空高频）：** "refused to divulge the source" — 拒绝透露消息来源，记者保护线人经典语境。对立词：withhold/conceal
- **商业/法律（GRE阅读）：** "the company divulged trade secrets to a competitor" — 泄露商业机密，与 confidentiality agreement 相对
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "he was famously tight-lipped, never divulging anything about his personal life" — 守口如瓶的角色

**画面：** Under relentless interrogation by a four-year-old who refused to let it go, the exhausted father accidentally divulged the truth about Santa Claus, and immediately realized he had just ruined the next ten Christmases.

**Synonyms:** disclose, reveal, expose, unveil, impart, broadcast, betray, leak
**Antonyms:** conceal, withhold, suppress, hide, cloak, keep secret

**区分：** disclose（更中性/正式，financial disclosure）| betray（含背叛/不忠，更强）| disseminate（广泛传播给大众）| divulge 独有"不该说却说"的违规感

---

## gloat

**释义：** 洋洋得意、幸灾乐祸地炫耀。别人惨的时候你显摆自己的好运——带有强烈负面色彩。

**词根：** 古诺斯语 glotta（咧嘴笑）。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑：** gloat over one's success / misfortune of others。对立词：commiserate（同情）、bemoan（哀叹）
- **对比考点：** easy commute → gloat vs hate commute → complain。gloat ≠ grumble（抱怨）

**Synonyms:** crow, boast, revel, exult, bask
**Antonyms:** commiserate, sympathize, bemoan

---

## circumspect

**释义：** 谨慎小心的、周到的。circum-（环绕）+ spect（看）= 环顾四周再行动。

**词根：** circum-（环绕）+ specere（看）。同根词：spectacle、inspect、prospect、retrospect。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** circumspect（谨慎） ↔ candid/reckless（坦诚/鲁莽）。"normally circumspect → unexpectedly candid" 是经典反差
- **人物刻画：** circumspect diplomat — 滴水不漏的外交官

**Synonyms:** cautious, wary, prudent, guarded, discreet
**Antonyms:** candid, reckless, rash, incautious, unwary

---

## candid

**释义：** 坦率的、直言不讳的。毫无保留地说真话。注意不要和 candor（名词）弄混。

**词根：** 拉丁语 candidus（白色的、纯净的）→ 白色象征纯洁无瑕 → 坦诚。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** candid ↔ circumspect/guarded — 坦诚↔谨慎
- **人物刻画：** candid admission = 坦率承认

**Synonyms:** frank, forthright, blunt, open, unreserved
**Antonyms:** guarded, circumspect, evasive, reticent

---

## synthesis

**释义：** 综合、合成。把独立元素整合为统一整体。GRE 常与 analysis（分析）对立——analysis 拆解，synthesis 捏合。

**词根：** 希腊语 synthesis = syn-（一起）+ tithenai（放置）= "放在一起"。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑对立：** synthesis ↔ analysis — 综合↔分析
- **学术语境：** a work of synthesis = 综合性著作（把多条线索编织为统一叙事）

**Synonyms:** integration, amalgamation, fusion, unification
**Antonyms:** analysis, separation, dissection

---

## contentious

**释义：** 有争议的、引起争论的；好争辩的。两层含义：(1) 话题本身有争议 (2) 人本身爱吵架。GRE 最常考第一义。

**词根：** 拉丁语 contentiosus ← contendere（争斗、争夺）= com-（一起）+ tendere（拉伸、用力）。同根词：contend（竞争；主张）、contention（争论；论点）、tend（趋向）、extend（延伸）、intend（打算）。

**场景：**
- **法律/政策（GRE阅读高频）：** "a contentious Supreme Court ruling" — 引发激烈争议的判决，对立词 unanimous/settled
- **人物刻画（GRE阅读）：** "a contentious personality who turned every staff meeting into a debate" — 刺头人物
- **填空逻辑对立：** scientists contentious ↔ laypeople unhesitating — 内行争议不休，外行毫不怀疑（真题经典对比）

**画面：** The condo HOA meeting, originally called to discuss the shade of beige for the lobby, devolved into a contentious three-hour war zone in which two neighbors hired lawyers, one threatened to run for board president purely out of spite, and someone's emotional-support parrot was cited for excessive profanity.

**Synonyms:** controversial, disputatious, argumentative, quarrelsome, polemical, combative, fractious
**Antonyms:** uncontroversial, settled, amicable, harmonious, conciliatory, irenic

**区分：** controversial（仅指话题有争议，不可形容人）| litigious（专指"好打官司的"，法律特化版）| pugnacious（想用拳头打架，contentious 想用嘴吵架）

---

## fraught

**释义：** 充满（问题/危险/紧张）的。⚠️ 不是"充满好的东西"——fraught with joy 是错误的，fraught with peril/difficulty 是对的。

**词根：** 中古荷兰语 vracht（货物）→ fraught = loaded（装载的）。最初中性义"装满的"已退化，现代英语中几乎只用于负面。

**场景：**
- **填空逻辑：** fraught history / fraught relationship → 充满矛盾的历史/关系
- **对比考点：** 与 contentious 近义配对 → fraught = 充满问题，contentious = 引起争议

**Synonyms:** tense, charged, troubled, laden (with problems)
**Antonyms:** calm, untroubled, smooth, serene

**区分：** ⚠️ fraught 在现代英语中几乎总是负面。"The negotiations were fraught" = 谈判充满紧张，不是"谈判内容很丰富"。

---

## illustrious

**释义：** *adj.* 著名的，杰出的，显赫的 — 形容人或事业因卓越成就而享有极高声望和光辉荣誉。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *illustris* = "lit up, bright, distinguished"，源自 *illustrare*（照亮、使明亮）。同根词：**illustrate**（阐明 → 用光"照亮"概念）、**luster**（光泽）、**lustrous**（有光泽的）。核心意象是"被光芒照耀的 → 耀眼的 → 杰出的"。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 人物刻画：** *The historian devoted an entire chapter to the general's illustrious but ultimately tragic career.* 描写历史名将的辉煌生涯，常与后续的转折（tragic, decline）形成对比。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *Though his early work was obscure, he later enjoyed an illustrious reputation.* 与 obscure（默默无闻）构成时间对比，填空经典对立。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 学术论证：** *She was the latest in an illustrious line of scholars at the university.* 形容学术世家或机构的传承荣耀，常见于阅读烘托出身。

**画面：** 🎭 Imagine a Nobel laureate stepping onto a brilliantly lit stage at Stockholm, the spotlight following every move — that glow of a lifetime of achievement is the essence of *illustrious*.

**Synonyms:** eminent, renowned, celebrated, distinguished, preeminent, luminous, glorious
**Antonyms:** obscure, unknown, ignominious, undistinguished, inglorious

**区分：**
- *illustrious* vs *lustrous* — 同根但完全不同：前指"有名望"，后指"有光泽的"（lustrous hair / pearls）
- *illustrious* vs *notorious* — 一褒一贬：illustrious = 光荣显赫，notorious = 臭名昭著
- *illustrious* vs *eminent* — 非常近义，但 illustrious 更强调"光辉"和"荣耀光环"，eminent 更中性偏"地位高"

---

## munificent

**释义：** *adj.* 极慷慨的，出手大方的 — 形容人在金钱/资源上极其大方，远超常态的慷慨（不止是 generous，而是 lavish generosity）。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *munificus* = *munus*（礼物、公共义务）+ *facere*（做、制造）= 字面义"制造礼物的人"。同源词：**munificence** (n. 慷慨)、**municipal**（市政的 ← 公共义务）、**remunerate**（酬劳 ← re-回 + munus-礼物）、**immune**（免除义务的 ← im-无 + munis-义务）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 人物刻画：** *The munificent donor's gift single-handedly funded the new library wing.* 形容慈善家/捐赠人的极度慷慨，常与 parsimonious/stingy 构成对比。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *Though personally frugal, she was munificent in her support of the arts.* frugal ↔ munificent 是经典对立：对己节俭，对人慷慨。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 历史评价：** *The monarch's munificent patronage attracted the finest artists and scholars to the court.* 形容君主/权贵的慷慨赞助，历史类文章常见。

**画面：** 💰 A reclusive billionaire who, without a word of publicity, pays off the student loans of an entire graduating class — not for recognition, but because they can. That silent, overwhelming generosity is *munificent*.

**Synonyms:** generous, bountiful, lavish, magnanimous, liberal, openhanded, beneficent, unstinting
**Antonyms:** stingy, parsimonious, miserly, niggardly, penurious, tightfisted, ungenerous

**区分：**
- *munificent* vs *generous* — generous 是普通"大方"，munificent 是"极其大方、挥金如土级别"，程度远超 generous
- *munificent* vs *magnanimous* — munificent 侧重**金钱/物质**层面的慷慨（giving money），magnanimous 侧重**精神/气度**层面的宽宏大量（forgiving enemies, noble in spirit）
- *munificent* vs *philanthropic* — philanthropic 强调"慈善/人道主义目的"，munificent 只强调"给的多"，不一定有慈善动机

---

## taciturn

**释义：** *adj.* 沉默寡言的，不爱说话的 — 形容天生性格沉默，习惯性少言寡语，不是一时不想说，而是一贯如此。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *taciturnus* ← *tacitus*（沉默的）← *tacere*（保持沉默）。同根词：**tacit**（adj. 心照不宣的、默示的 — tacit agreement）、**taciturnity**（n. 沉默寡言）、**reticent**（re-再 + tacere → 缄默的）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 人物刻画：** *The taciturn farmer answered every question with a monosyllable, if he answered at all.* 形容人沉默成性，阅读/填空中常作为"话少"的极端表达。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *Though taciturn in private, the professor became remarkably voluble in the lecture hall.* taciturn ↔ voluble/loquacious/garrulous 是 GRE 最高频对立词群：沉默寡言 ↔ 话多。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 文学评论：** *The taciturn protagonist's emotional depth is revealed through action rather than dialogue.* 文学人物分析中，沉默型主角通过行动而非语言展露内心。

**画面：** 🤐 The old rancher who sits on his porch every evening, and when a neighbor waves and asks how he's doing, he just tips his hat and says "Fine." — one word, every time, for forty years. That is *taciturn*.

**Synonyms:** reticent, laconic, reserved, silent, uncommunicative, tight-lipped, closemouthed
**Antonyms:** loquacious, garrulous, voluble, talkative, effusive, chatty, verbose

**区分：**
- *taciturn* vs *reticent* — **GRE 高频陷阱！** taciturn = 天性沉默寡言（habitual silence, by nature），reticent = 对某话题刻意缄口不谈（unwilling to speak about a specific topic）。一个人可以 reticent about his past but not taciturn in daily life.
- *taciturn* vs *laconic* — laconic = 用极少词表达（like Spartans），侧重**简洁**；taciturn = 根本就不说话，侧重**沉默**
- *taciturn* vs *tacit* — tacit (adj.) = 不用语言表达的、心照不宣的（tacit consent 默许），共享词根但意思完全不同

---

## arduous

**释义：** *adj.* 艰难的，费力的，艰巨的 — 形容任务/过程极其费力，像爬陡坡一样每一步都在消耗大量精力。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *arduus* = "steep, high, difficult to climb"（陡峭的、高耸的、难以攀爬的）。核心意象从古罗马地理而来——爬起来很陡的山 → 引申为任何事情"费力难做"。同根词：**ardor**（n. 热情，字面义"燃烧"——另一拉丁词根 *ardere* 燃烧，别搞混）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 任务/过程：** *The arduous trek through the Himalayas tested even the most experienced climbers.* 形容体力/精神上极度费力的任务，与 facile/effortless 形成对立。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *What appeared to be a simple task proved far more arduous than anticipated.* 简单表象 ↔ 艰难现实，填空经典转折。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 学术/历史：** *The committee faced the arduous task of reconciling decades of contradictory data.* 学术论证中形容"耗时费力"的整合/梳理工作。

**画面：** 🏔️ You're halfway up a scree-covered slope at 14,000 feet — every step forward slides you half a step back, your lungs burn, and the summit still looks impossibly far. That grinding, gasping effort is *arduous*.

**Synonyms:** strenuous, laborious, grueling, onerous, taxing, demanding, punishing, backbreaking
**Antonyms:** easy, effortless, facile, simple, undemanding, light, painless

**区分：**
- *arduous* vs *onerous* — arduous 侧重**体力/精神上的费力**（physically/mentally demanding），onerous 侧重**负担/义务的沉重**（burdensome obligation，如 onerous contract terms）。GRE 中 onerous 更偏"法律/财务重负"。
- *arduous* vs *laborious* — laborious 强调**耗时长+枯燥**（slow, tedious, requiring many steps），arduous 强调**每一步都难**（steep difficulty）。抄写整本字典是 laborious，爬珠峰是 arduous。
- *arduous* vs *grueling* — grueling = 极其 punishing/exhausting，程度比 arduous 更重，带有"折磨人"的意味

---

## inchoate

**释义：** *adj.* 初期的，未成形的，混乱不完整的 — 刚刚开始但杂乱无章、结构不清，尚未形成明确形态。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *inchoatus* ← *inchoare* = "to begin, to hitch up"（套上犁具 → 开始耕作）。意象：刚套上牛犁，田还没翻 → 才开始，混乱无序。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 想法/情感：** *His political views remained inchoate, a jumble of contradictions rather than a coherent ideology.* inchoate ↔ coherent/organized — 零乱 ↔ 条理清晰。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 学术论证：** *The field was still in its inchoate phase, with competing theories and no agreed-upon methodology.* 形容学科早期混沌状态。

**画面：** 🎨 A canvas with a few random brushstrokes — blue here, red there — no one, not even the painter, can tell what it's going to be. That messy, unformed beginning is *inchoate*.

**Synonyms:** rudimentary, amorphous, formless, embryonic, nascent, undeveloped
**Antonyms:** developed, mature, organized, coherent, fully-formed

**区分：**
- *inchoate* vs *incipient* — inchoate = 结构混乱的初期（disorganized），incipient = 方向已明的初期（identifiable but small）。inchoate ideas（一团乱麻的想法）vs incipient rebellion（刚萌芽但已清晰的叛乱）
- *inchoate* vs *nascent* — nascent 只强调"刚诞生"，中性；inchoate 强调"未成形+混乱"

---

## incipient

**释义：** *adj.* 初期的，刚开始出现的 — 刚刚开始显现，但结构已清晰可辨，只是规模/程度尚小。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *incipere* = *in-*（里）+ *capere*（拿取）= to begin, to take in hand。同根词：**inception**（开端）、**incipient**（刚开始的）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 趋势/变化：** *The incipient rebellion, though small, alarmed the authorities enough to prompt a crackdown.* 刚冒头但方向已明，只是规模小。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 医学/科学：** *The drug slowed the progression of incipient Alzheimer's disease.* 疾病"早期/初期"，病理清晰可辨。

**画面：** 🌱 A tiny green shoot poking through the soil — you can already tell it's going to be a tomato plant, not a weed. It's small but its identity is clear. That's *incipient*.

**Synonyms:** nascent, emerging, budding, beginning, initial, embryonic
**Antonyms:** mature, full-blown, developed, terminal

**区分：**
- *incipient* vs *inchoate* — incipient = 方向已明的初期，inchoate = 混乱未成形的初期
- *incipient* vs *nascent* — 非常近义，incipient 强调"开始显现（通常指不好的事）"，常带 ominous 意味

---

## enmity

**释义：** *n.* 敌意，仇恨，敌对 — 人与人/群体/国家之间的深刻敌意和仇视，比 hostility 更持久、更根深蒂固。

**词根：** 古法语 *enemistié* ← 拉丁语 *inimicus* = *in-*（不）+ *amicus*（朋友）= 不是朋友 → 敌人。同根词：**enemy**（n. 敌人）、**inimical**（adj. 敌意的、不利的）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 人际关系：** *Years of professional rivalry had hardened into personal enmity.* 竞争升级为仇恨，与 amity（友好）构成填空经典对立。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 历史/政治：** *The centuries-old enmity between the two nations thwarted every peace negotiation.* 世仇/民族仇恨阻碍和平进程，历史政治文章高频。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *The two leaders, once bound by enmity, eventually forged an unlikely friendship.* enmity ↔ friendship/amity — 仇恨 ↔ 友谊。

**画面：** ⚔️ Two families who haven't spoken in three generations — nobody even remembers what started it, but the cold glare across the village square never wavers. That fossilized hatred is *enmity*.

**Synonyms:** hostility, animosity, antagonism, rancor, antipathy, ill will, acrimony
**Antonyms:** amity, friendship, goodwill, camaraderie, affinity, concord

**区分：**
- *enmity* vs *animosity* — enmity 更**长期/深层/结构性**（如民族世仇），animosity 可指较**局部/临时的敌意**（如同事间摩擦）
- *enmity* vs *hostility* — hostility 可指一时的敌对行为/态度，enmity 指深入骨髓的仇恨状态
- *enmity* vs *antipathy* — antipathy = 天生的反感/厌恶（gut-level dislike），enmity = 长期积累的仇恨（built over time）

## chicanery

**释义：** *n.* 诡计，欺骗，花招 — 尤指法律/政治中用花言巧语、钻空子、似是而非的论调来欺骗，不是直白的谎言而是"技术性耍诈"。

**词根：** 法语 *chicaner* = "to quibble, to split hairs"（狡辩、抠字眼）。17世纪进入英语，特指法律诉讼中用程序把戏拖延/欺骗对方。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 法律/政治：** *The senator's defense of the tax loophole was pure chicanery, cloaked in the language of economic growth.* chicanery ↔ honesty/candor。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 司法/商业：** *The court sanctioned the attorney for engaging in chicanery rather than presenting a legitimate argument.* 法律语境中的"狡辩/程序欺诈"。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *What he called "creative accounting" was, in the auditor's view, outright chicanery.* 美化标签 ↔ 真实本质。

**画面：** 🦊 A lawyer who knows his client is guilty, so instead of arguing innocence, he spends six months filing motions about the font size on the search warrant — and gets the case dismissed. That's *chicanery*.

**Synonyms:** trickery, deception, sophistry, subterfuge, duplicity, casuistry, legerdemain, skulduggery
**Antonyms:** honesty, forthrightness, candor, probity, straightforwardness, integrity

**区分：**
- *chicanery* vs *sophistry* — sophistry = 看似有理实则谬误的论证（哲学逻辑层面），chicanery = 利用规则漏洞/程序伎俩欺骗（法律/政治实操）
- *chicanery* vs *duplicity* — duplicity = 表里不一的欺骗（double-dealing），chicanery = 技术性狡辩/钻空子
- *chicanery* vs *subterfuge* — subterfuge = 任何形式的规避/托词（广），chicanery = 法律政治语境中的诡辩（窄）

## raillery

**释义：** *n.* 善意的揶揄，戏谑，打趣 — 朋友间不带恶意的开玩笑，带着好感的调侃。

**词根：** 法语 *railler* = "to mock, to tease"（嘲笑、打趣）。17世纪进入英语，始终保留"善意玩笑"基调。注意：*rail* (v. 怒骂) 不同词源，不要混淆。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 社交互动：** *The dinner party was filled with witty raillery, the guests trading affectionate jabs across the table.* 轻松的社交调侃，与 acerbity/derision 构成对立。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 文学人物：** *The author's raillery, never cruel, revealed a deep fondness for his subjects.* 文学评论中作者对笔下人物的"善意打趣"。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *What she dismissed as harmless raillery, he perceived as cutting derision.* raillery ↔ derision — 善意玩笑 ↔ 恶意嘲讽。

**画面：** 🍻 Two old friends at a bar, one says "Remember when you tripped on stage at graduation?" — the other laughs louder than anyone, then fires back "Remember when you set the kitchen on fire making toast?" Both grinning. That warm, needle-free trading of jabs is *raillery*.

**Synonyms:** banter, teasing, badinage, persiflage, jesting, repartee, joshing
**Antonyms:** flattery, seriousness, solemnity, gravity, insult

**区分：**
- *raillery* vs *derision* — 填空关键对立！raillery = 善意玩笑（affectionate teasing），derision = 恶意嘲讽（contemptuous mockery）
- *raillery* vs *banter* — banter 更日常"斗嘴/吐槽"，raillery 更正式/文学化
- *raillery* vs *rail* — rail (v.) = 怒骂、咆哮指责，与 raillery 完全无关

---

## sage

**释义：** *n.* 智者，贤人 / *adj.* 睿智的，贤明的 — 因阅历深厚而见解超凡。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *sapere* = "to taste, to be wise"（品尝 → 辨别 → 智慧）→ 古法语 *sage*。同根词：**sagacious**、**sagacity**、**sapient**、**savor**（品味，同源"品尝"义）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 人物刻画：** *The CEO sought the advice of the company's sage founder before making the acquisition.* sage counsel — 德高望重的智慧长者。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 历史/哲学：** *Confucius is often depicted as a wandering sage imparting wisdom to disciples.* 圣贤形象。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** sage ↔ foolish/ignorant/charlatan

**画面：** 🧙 The village elder who listens to three hours of heated argument, then says one sentence that makes everyone go silent — and they all realize that's the answer. *Sage*.

**Synonyms:** (n.) philosopher, guru, pundit, savant, luminary; (adj.) wise, judicious, prudent, sagacious, discerning
**Antonyms:** (n.) fool, charlatan, ignoramus; (adj.) foolish, unwise, imprudent

**区分：**
- *sage* vs *guru* — guru 带精神导师/宗教色彩，sage 更中性偏"阅历深厚的长者智囊"
- *sage* (n.) vs *sagacity* (n.) — sage = 人，sagacity = 品质

## sagacity

**释义：** *n.* 睿智，洞察力，远见卓识 — 敏锐判断力+实践经验结合的智慧，不是书本知识而是"看得透"的能力。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *sapere* = to taste, to be wise + *-acity* 名词后缀"品质"。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 能力评价：** *The detective's sagacity was legendary — she could unravel a case from a single overlooked detail.* 从细微线索洞察全局的敏锐。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 商业/政治：** *The investor's sagacity in predicting the market crash earned him a fortune.* 远见/先见之明。

**画面：** 🔍 Sherlock Holmes glancing at a stranger for two seconds and rattling off their entire life story — all from a smudge on the collar. That uncanny penetration is *sagacity*.

**Synonyms:** discernment, perspicacity, acumen, shrewdness, astuteness, insight, wisdom
**Antonyms:** folly, foolishness, obtuseness, shortsightedness, ignorance

**区分：**
- *sagacity* vs *sapience* — sagacity = 实践智慧+敏锐判断（street smart），sapience = 理论/哲学智慧（deep thinking）
- *sagacity* vs *perspicacity* — 非常近义；perspicacity 偏向"穿透表象的洞察"，sagacity 偏向"基于经验的明智判断"

## expunge

**释义：** *v.* 删除，抹去，擦掉 — 从记录/记忆中彻底消除，不留痕迹。比 delete 更正式，常用于法律/正式文书语境。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *expungere* = *ex-*（出）+ *pungere*（刺、点）= 古罗马编辑在要删除的文字下打点标记 → 彻底删除。同根词：**puncture**（刺破）、**pungent**（辛辣刺鼻的）、**compunction**（良心刺痛）、**poignant**（尖锐辛酸的）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 法律/记录：** *The judge ordered the conviction expunged from his record, as if it had never occurred.* 法律语境中从档案中彻底抹去。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *The dictator sought to expunge all traces of dissent, yet underground resistance persisted.* expunge ↔ preserve/retain。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 历史/记忆：** *The regime attempted to expunge the massacre from the nation's collective memory.* 从历史记忆中抹去。

**画面：** 🧹 A whiteboard with a single name written on it — the teacher erases it so thoroughly that not even a smudge remains, as though the name was never there.

**Synonyms:** erase, delete, obliterate, efface, eradicate, annihilate, excise, strike out
**Antonyms:** preserve, retain, insert, add, record, inscribe

**区分：**
- *expunge* vs *expurgate* — expunge = 彻底删除不留痕迹（erase completely），expurgate = 选择性删改不当内容保留主体（censor/cleanse）。expunge a criminal record vs expurgate a novel for schools
- *expunge* vs *efface* — efface = 擦去表面痕迹使之模糊（self-effacing = 谦逊低调），expunge = 从记录中正式/彻底删除（更多法律/正式语境）
- *expunge* vs *excise* — excise = 切除/删除（像外科手术切掉一部分），expunge = 抹去/删除（像擦掉）

## factionalism

**释义：** *n.* 派系主义，内斗 — 组织/政党/团体内部因派系分裂而产生对立和内耗。

**词根：** 拉丁语 *factio* = "a political party, a group acting together" ← *facere* (to do)。faction → factional → factionalism。同根词：**faction**（派系）、**factious**（adj. 好内斗的）。

**场景：**
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 政治/组织：** *The party's factionalism paralyzed decision-making, as each wing blocked the other's proposals.* factionalism ↔ unity/cohesion。
- 🔹 **GRE 阅读 · 历史分析：** *Historians attribute the empire's decline more to internal factionalism than to external threats.* 内部分裂 vs 外部威胁。
- 🔹 **GRE 填空 · 逻辑对立：** *The leader's appeal for unity was undermined by rampant factionalism within the ranks.* factionalism ↔ unity/solidarity。

**画面：** 🏛️ A political party with 12 members and 13 subcommittees — every meeting devolves into three hours of procedural knife-fighting over who chairs what, while the actual agenda gathers dust.

**Synonyms:** divisiveness, sectarianism, infighting, partisanship, discord, schism
**Antonyms:** unity, solidarity, cohesion, harmony, concord, consensus

**区分：**
- *factionalism* vs *sectarianism* — factionalism = 任何组织内的派系内斗，sectarianism = 特指宗教/教派之间的对立冲突
- *factionalism* vs *partisanship* — partisanship = 盲目的党派忠诚（支持己方不管对错），factionalism = 派系分裂和内斗（多方互斗而非二元对立）
