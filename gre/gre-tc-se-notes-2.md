### ✅ [Easy] [TC3] test 1 s1 #3 — origin + produce + eaten (BEG)

> The skin of the poison dart frog contains deadly poisons called batrachotoxins. But the (i)[------] of the toxins has remained an enigma, as the frog does not (ii)[------] them. Now an analysis suggests that the melyrid beetle is the source. Collected beetle specimens all contained batrachotoxins, suggesting that these beetles are (iii)[------] by the frogs.

- ✅ BEG · ❌ none
- **信号：** enigma → 线索：来源成谜 → 因为蛙不产毒 → 甲虫含毒 → 蛙吃甲虫
- **逻辑：** 因果链（as → suggesting that）

**各词释义：**
```
A. effect 效果   | B. origin 来源        | C. purpose 目的    
D. pressure 压力 | E. produce 产生       | F. suffer from 遭受
G. eaten 被吃    | H. neutralized 被中和 | I. poisoned 被毒害 
```

### ❌ [Medium] [TC] test 9 s2 #2 — derivative + remarkably pioneering (CD) · answered BD

> The description of Green's scholarship as (i)[------] is grossly misleading: while her research on interstellar particles is not especially novel, the conclusions she draws from her data are (ii)[------].

- ✅ CD (derivative + remarkably pioneering) · ❌ BD (tendentious + remarkably pioneering)
- **信号：** grossly misleading → 描述是错的。冒号解释：research 不新颖（让步），but conclusions are 开创性（转折）→ 只盯不新颖是片面的
- **逻辑：** 转折让步（while）+ 解释说明（冒号）

**各词释义：**
```
A. esoteric 深奥难懂的                | B. tendentious 偏颇的               | C. derivative 派生的                    
D. remarkably pioneering 显著开创性的 | E. dubiously supported 可疑地支撑的 | F. strangely comforting 奇怪地令人安心的
```

### ❌ [Medium] [SE] test 10 s1 #4 — expansion + augmentation (BD) · answered BE

> Though it may seem as if more than a century of [------] has made the electrical grid an all-encompassing web connecting the whole of the continent, many vast and beautiful areas remain without power.

- ✅ BD · ❌ BE
- **信号：** Though it may seem → 退一步承认。has made the grid all-encompassing → 什么让电网全覆？扩张。yet → 仍有地区没电
- **逻辑：** 转折让步
- SE 近义对：expansion（扩张）+ augmentation（增大）= 规模增长
- ⚠️ BE 错因：omnipresence 是结果不是原因，"a century of omnipresence" 逻辑倒置

**各词释义：**
```
A. refinement 精炼   | B. expansion 扩张        | C. ubiquity 无处不在
D. augmentation 增大 | E. omnipresence 无所不在 | F. isolation 隔离   
```

### ❌ [Hard] [TC] test 27 s2 #1 — inevitable (E) · answered D

> Consolidating memory is not instantaneous or even [------]: every memory must be encoded and moved from short-term to long-term storage, and some of these memories are, for whatever reason, more vividly imprinted than others.

- ✅ E (inevitable) · ❌ D (momentary)
- **信号：** "not A or even B" = 递进（A 不够，B 更甚）。instantaneous → 甚至不必然。冒号解释必须经过编码转移，且有的记忆更鲜活 → 不必然成功
- **逻辑：** 递进（not...or even...）
- ⚠️ D 错因：momentary 和 instantaneous 同向同程度，构不成递进

**各词释义：**
```
A. salutary 有益的 | B. deliberate 故意的 | C. sequential 顺序的 | D. momentary 短暂的 | E. inevitable 必然的
```

### ✅ [Easy] [TC3] test 1 s2 #4 — competitive + wide-ranging + comprehensive (AEG)

> Now that photographic prints have become a popular field for collecting, auctions are becoming more (i)[------]. It is not just the entry of new collectors into the field that is causing this intensification. Established collectors' interests are also becoming more (ii)[------]. ```

### ❌ [Medium] [SE] test 100 s1 #2 — corruption + integrity (BE) · answered CE

> The widespread (i)[------] that has accompanied economic growth is not the result of the people's adopting the leader's own standards. Her personal (ii)[------] is recognized by all.


### ✅ [Hard] [SE] test 93 s2 #6 — exasperating + frustrating (AF)

> Given that the detailed scientific description of four-legged animals' walking gaits is now over a century old, it is [------] that so many artists, taxidermists, and others responsible for depicting animals get leg positions wrong.

- ✅ AF (exasperating + frustrating) · ❌ none
- **信号：** Given that...over a century old → 都一百多年了还错 → 恼火
- **逻辑：** 因果（Given that → 预期应掌握，现实相反 → 令人沮丧）
- SE 近义对：exasperating（令人恼火的）+ frustrating（令人沮丧的）= 负面情绪

**各词释义：**
```
A. exasperating 令人恼火的   | B. unsurprising 不意外的     | C. satisfying 令人满意的  
D. predictable 可预测的       | E. remarkable 非凡的         | F. frustrating 令人沮丧的 
```
